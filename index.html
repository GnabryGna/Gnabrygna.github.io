I'm a master's student in Artificial Intelligence at Hanyang University, where I work as a graduate researcher at COLAB.

Before that, I studied Multimedia Engineering at Dongguk University – and along the way, was an undergraduate research assistant at SI Lab.

My research interests are vision-language-action models, sim-to-real transfer, and robot manipulation.
