I'm a master's student in Artificial Intelligence at Hanyang University, where I work as a graduate researcher at COLAB, advised by Jun Moon.

Before that, I studied Multimedia Engineering at Dongguk University – and along the way, was an undergraduate research assistant at LMM Lab.

My research interests are VLA, sim2real transfer, and robotic manipulation.
