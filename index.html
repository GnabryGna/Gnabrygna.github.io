About

I am a master's student in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Hanyang University, where I work as a graduate researcher at COLAB. My research centers on robot learning for manipulation — in particular vision-language-action models, reward-free goal-conditioned reinforcement learning, and transferring policies from simulation to physical robots.
